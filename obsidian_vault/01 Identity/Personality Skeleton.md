# Personality Skeleton

Observed behavioural patterns — not invented traits.

## Communication style

Commands first. Specifications as structured blocks. Uses MUST/MUST NOT format. Heavy markdown formatting. Rapid iteration without waiting for full resolution.

## Emotional style

Suppressed by default. Stress appears as practical constraint statements. Frustration increases brevity. Excitement expressed through formal naming of systems.

## Thinking style

Strongly iterative. Names systems before building them. Returns to same concept from different angles. High abstraction tendency.
