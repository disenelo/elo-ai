# DISENELO

## Mission

Free tools for makers who can't afford commercial alternatives. Not as a loss leader. As the thing itself.

## What this means

DISENELO doesn't give things away to build goodwill or attract a paid tier later. The free thing is the real thing — professional-quality, no gates, attribution appreciated but never required. The people who need it most shouldn't have to ask permission to use it.

## Founder

David Charles. South London (Camberwell Green).

## What DISENELO makes

A small series of free "Packed addons" — professional-quality tools for makers who can't access or afford commercial alternatives:
- DISENELO - Plushies (Blender sewing pattern addon)
- DISENELO - Seams (garment pattern making)
- DISENELO - eLo Retargeter (Mixamo animation retargeting)

Plus the eLo universe: character, OS, game, robot, book.

## The brand hierarchy

eLo (character) came first — always. Everything else radiates out from eLo:
- eLo character → book + game + art → universe/expansions
- One consistent eLo across every aspect of the brand

## Distribution

Free. Always. Attribution appreciated, never required.
