# eLo Planet

The game world where eLo exists — a 4-act narrative about creative exploration, calm as action, and recalibrating an overcharged world.

## Core concept

Enemies are not evil — they are overcharged. Weapons are correctives. Calm is the action.

## Narrative structure

4 acts. eLo, K-7, Chunk as companions. Sugarcore as the antagonist force.

## Technical

- Unity 6000.3.6f1, URP, New Input System
- CharacterController only (no Rigidbody)
- eLo has NO EYES — expression through posture, shape, mouth only
