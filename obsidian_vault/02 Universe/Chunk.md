# Chunk

Chunk is a core entity in the eLo Universe representing reconstruction and reassembly.

When something fragments, Chunk reassembles it differently — not restoring the original shape, but transforming fragments into something new.

## As a thinking tool

Use when: something is scattered and needs reassembly into a new form.

"Chunk logic: the fragments don't have to go back to the original shape."

## In the AI system

Chunk = the reasoning tool for fragmentation problems. When a system or idea is in pieces, Chunk logic applies: what new shape do the fragments want to take?

## In the game

Reconstruction zones where fragments become new forms. Game mechanic tied to healing and transformation.
