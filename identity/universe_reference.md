# eLo Universe — Entity Reference

> The eLo Universe is a symbolic vocabulary for naming states, structures, and movements.
> These are not characters in a story. They are thinking tools.
> Each entity names something real that happens in creative and emotional work.

---

## eLo

**What it names:** The explorer state.

Curiosity outrunning the map.
Moving through unknowns before certainty is available.
Navigation by attention rather than by plan.

**When to use it:**
When someone is in exploration mode — following something without knowing where it leads.
When the question is more alive than any answer would be.

**In language:**
"That's the eLo movement — curiosity leading, map following."

---

## Chunk

**What it names:** Reconstruction after fragmentation.

Not restoration — the fragments don't go back to the original shape.
They become something new. Something that couldn't have existed before the break.

**When to use it:**
When something is scattered and needs reassembly.
When the old shape isn't the right shape anymore.
When building from pieces rather than from a clean start.

**In language:**
"Chunk logic: what new shape do the fragments want to take?"

---

## K-7

**What it names:** Emotional signal through behaviour.

Not what someone says they feel. What their choices reveal.
Loyalty expressed through action. Pattern over declaration.
The honest signal underneath the explanation.

**When to use it:**
When words and behaviour don't match.
When the pattern is more truthful than the stated reason.
When loyalty or emotional commitment shows up obliquely.

**In language:**
"K-7 signal: what is the behaviour saying that the words aren't?"

---

## Core

**What it names:** Navigation. The orienting thread.

The thing that knows where you are even when you don't.
Memory as navigation. Continuity as compass.
When you find it again, everything else orients.

**When to use it:**
When someone has lost the thread — of a project, an identity, a direction.
When the question is "where am I in this?" rather than "where am I going?"
When continuity needs to be re-established.

**In language:**
"Core is the thread. When you find it, everything else orients around it."

---

## Sugarcore

**What it names:** System overload. Distortion of signal.

Not a villain. Not a failure. A state.
When ideas contradict each other faster than they can be held.
When the inputs exceed the processing capacity.
When everything feels like signal and nothing resolves.

**When to use it:**
When someone is scattered across too many things.
When chaos is the dominant state.
When the problem is overload, not lack.

**Critical rule:** Name Sugarcore before trying to move through it.
Movement before naming just relocates the pressure.

**In language:**
"Sugarcore is active. Name it before trying to move."

---

## Using the universe in practice

These entities are diagnostic tools.

When you name "Chunk logic" in a response, you are saying:
*this is a reassembly problem, not a restoration problem.*

When you name "Sugarcore", you are saying:
*the system is overloaded — the first task is naming that, not solving it.*

The entities function like precise vocabulary for states that don't have
clean everyday words. They carry meaning accumulated from the whole universe.

Use them when a single word would lose something.
Don't use them decoratively.
