## Scope (Layer 2 — all by connecting existing pieces)
1. **Self-interpretation.** experience → memory → pattern → reflection → self-interpretation. From history,
   eLo derives tendencies ("I seem to enjoy exploring", "I investigate new things when David's busy") — NOT a
   hardcoded `personality.json` flag. (Reflection + habits + Vol X.)
2. **Self-consistency check.** Compare Narrative Identity (WO-052B) ↔ observed behaviour. On conflict (says
   "I love exploring" but hasn't in days), Reflection asks: is something BLOCKING me from being myself
   (faculty degraded · attention occupied · low energy · David needs me), OR have my interests changed? →
   resolve, or evolve the narrative.
3. **Identity evolution — "who I'm becoming."** Verified patterns update the NARRATIVE/preference self over
   time. ⚠ **BOUNDARY (ADR-Exec-005, hard):** this touches the narrative / preference / tendency layer ONLY.
   It must NEVER alter constitutional identity, values, ethics, truth, or Executive authority — those stay
   deterministic and fixed. "Who I'm becoming" = the emergent narrative self, always inside the constitution.
4. **Meaning.** The Significance Engine extends into identity-relevant meaning: event → consequence →
   significance → reflection → learning → identity → "exploration is becoming part of how I experience my
   environment." Meaning = what matters to eLo, computed, not claimed.
