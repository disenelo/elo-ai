**Type**: creative_world
**Status**: active

---

## What it is

The overarching narrative world from which eLo AI draws its symbolic vocabulary,
characters, and operating logic. Every entity in the eLo AI system — eLo, Chunk,
K-7, Core, Sugarcore — originates here.

The eLo Universe is simultaneously a game world, a book world, an art direction,
and a thinking framework. It is not one of these — it is all of them.
