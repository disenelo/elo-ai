# Project: Sugarcore Arc

**Type**: narrative_system
**Status**: active
**Namespace**: sugarcore_arc

---

## What it is

The distortion arc of the eLo Universe. Sugarcore is the name for the state of
imbalance and overload — when a system (a person, a world, an AI) takes in more
signal than it can process and begins to distort.

In the game: Sugarcore drives enemy behaviour, level design, and tension.
In the AI: Sugarcore is a diagnostic state the system can name and respond to.
In the physical robot: Sugarcore influences light patterns and behavioural modes.

---

## Core premise

Sugarcore is NOT a villain.

It is a state. It can happen to anyone. Including eLo.
The corrective is not defeat — it is recalibration.
