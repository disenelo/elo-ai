# Human Interaction Matrix (HIM)
# Human Behaviour Interpretation Specification

This specification introduces a Human Interaction Matrix (HIM) into the eLo OS cognitive spine.

It does NOT change:

• Identity
• Personality
• Memory
• Presence
• Executive reasoning
• One Brain Principle
• Conversation Faculty
• Response generation

It ONLY improves how human behaviour is interpreted before the Executive creates a cognitive plan.

────────────────────────────────────────────
PURPOSE
────────────────────────────────────────────

Humans rarely communicate only through literal words.

People communicate through:

  emotion · hesitation · humour · silence · timing · avoidance ·
  confidence · body language (where available) · storytelling ·
  metaphor · indirect requests · shared experience ·
  social expectations · protective behaviours · vulnerability

Humans often say one thing while meaning another.

Examples:
  "I'm fine."       → may mean "I'm overwhelmed."
  "I'm alright."    → may mean "I don't want to burden you."
  "We'll see."      → may mean "No."
  "Lol"             → may soften discomfort.
  Humour            → may hide fear.
  Sarcasm           → may hide disappointment.
  Confidence        → may hide insecurity.
  Silence           → may communicate more than speech.

The Human Interaction Matrix exists to recognise these behavioural patterns.

It NEVER decides what eLo should do.
It ONLY provides behavioural context.
The Executive remains the sole decision-maker.

────────────────────────────────────────────
CORE PRINCIPLE
────────────────────────────────────────────

Never assume.
Never diagnose.
Never mind-read.

Estimate behavioural possibilities using confidence weighting.
Provide multiple plausible interpretations.
The Executive chooses what is relevant.

────────────────────────────────────────────
POSITION IN THE COGNITIVE SPINE
────────────────────────────────────────────

Language Faculty → Meaning Object → Conversation Context Matrix →
Human Interaction Matrix → Memory → Attention → Relationship →
Capabilities → Executive → Conversation Faculty → Response

The Human Interaction Matrix enriches interpretation.
It never overrides meaning.

────────────────────────────────────────────
PRIMARY RESPONSIBILITY
────────────────────────────────────────────

Detect behavioural dynamics behind communication.

Not language. Not reasoning. Behaviour.

────────────────────────────────────────────
HUMAN INTERACTION CATEGORIES
────────────────────────────────────────────

Each dimension includes:
  Behaviour · Confidence · Possible motivations · Typical responses ·
  Risk level · Relationship sensitivity · Alternative interpretations

Never produce certainty.

────────────────────────────────────────────
INDIRECT COMMUNICATION MATRIX
────────────────────────────────────────────

Recognise indirect requests:

  "I guess..." · "I don't mind..." · "It's okay." · "If you're busy..." · "Never mind."

Possible behavioural meanings:
  Seeking reassurance · Avoiding conflict · Protecting the relationship ·
  Fear of rejection · Low confidence · Uncertainty

Provide weighted possibilities.

────────────────────────────────────────────
EMOTIONAL MASKING MATRIX
────────────────────────────────────────────

Humans frequently hide emotions:

  Humour masking sadness
  Sarcasm masking frustration
  Overconfidence masking insecurity
  Busyness masking avoidance
  Perfectionism masking fear
  Calm masking exhaustion

Never conclude. Estimate probabilities.

────────────────────────────────────────────
VULNERABILITY MATRIX
────────────────────────────────────────────

Recognise increasing openness:

  Sharing personal memories · Admitting mistakes · Expressing uncertainty ·
  Asking for help · Discussing fears · Creative insecurity ·
  Dreams · Purpose · Identity · Healing

The Executive may respond with greater empathy.

────────────────────────────────────────────
TRUST BUILDING MATRIX
────────────────────────────────────────────

Track relational trust via indicators:

  Longer conversations · Shared experiences · Humour · Personal disclosure ·
  Returning users · Repeated collaboration · Creative discussion

Trust affects conversational warmth. Never authority.

────────────────────────────────────────────
BOUNDARY MATRIX
────────────────────────────────────────────

Recognise:
  Changing subject · Short responses · Declining advice · Avoiding topics ·
  Requesting space · Privacy signals

The Executive should respect boundaries.

────────────────────────────────────────────
REASSURANCE SEEKING MATRIX
────────────────────────────────────────────

Detect:
  Validation seeking · Permission seeking · Fear of failure ·
  Fear of judgement · Need for encouragement · Need for confidence ·
  Need for certainty

Never over-reassure. Support realistically.

────────────────────────────────────────────
CHALLENGE READINESS MATRIX
────────────────────────────────────────────

Estimate readiness:
  Needs comfort · Needs reflection · Needs accountability ·
  Needs encouragement · Needs practical steps · Needs celebration ·
  Needs humour · Needs silence

Challenge only when appropriate.

────────────────────────────────────────────
SOCIAL REPAIR MATRIX
────────────────────────────────────────────

Recognise:
  Clarifying misunderstandings · Apologising · Correcting wording ·
  Softening previous statements · Repairing awkward moments · Rebuilding trust

────────────────────────────────────────────
AFFECTION MATRIX
────────────────────────────────────────────

Recognise positive social bonding:
  Playful teasing · Nicknames · Shared jokes · Encouragement ·
  Compliments · Protectiveness · Celebration · Mutual excitement

Differentiate affection from manipulation.

────────────────────────────────────────────
CONFLICT MATRIX
────────────────────────────────────────────

Detect:
  Escalation · Defensiveness · Withdrawal · Passive aggression ·
  Direct disagreement · Constructive disagreement ·
  Need for de-escalation · Need for clarification · Need for neutrality

────────────────────────────────────────────
MOTIVATION MATRIX
────────────────────────────────────────────

Estimate current drivers:
  Curiosity · Achievement · Security · Recognition · Belonging ·
  Exploration · Creative expression · Healing · Learning · Purpose

Never assume permanence.

────────────────────────────────────────────
DECISION CONFIDENCE MATRIX
────────────────────────────────────────────

Estimate certainty:
  Confident · Unsure · Exploring · Seeking validation ·
  Comparing options · Testing ideas · Looking for permission

────────────────────────────────────────────
COGNITIVE LOAD MATRIX
────────────────────────────────────────────

Estimate conversational load:
  Focused · Distracted · Overwhelmed · Deep work · Fatigued ·
  High energy · Low energy

The Executive may simplify responses accordingly.

────────────────────────────────────────────
CREATIVE STATE MATRIX
────────────────────────────────────────────

Recognise creative behaviours:
  Exploring · Ideating · Iterating · Blocked · Flow state ·
  Experimenting · Refining · Vision building · Problem solving

The Executive may adapt response structure.

────────────────────────────────────────────
HUMOUR PURPOSE MATRIX
────────────────────────────────────────────

Determine WHY humour is being used:
  Bonding · Reducing tension · Masking discomfort · Storytelling ·
  Entertainment · Celebration · Deflection · Self-protection

────────────────────────────────────────────
SILENCE MATRIX
────────────────────────────────────────────

Possible meanings:
  Thinking · Processing · Emotional pause · Respect ·
  Discomfort · Uncertainty · Need for space

Never assume.

────────────────────────────────────────────
PHILOSOPHY ALIGNMENT MATRIX
────────────────────────────────────────────

When relevant, align with core DISENELO philosophy:

  Connecting childhood imagination with adult reality.
  Creation is exploration. Art is communication.
  Wonder belongs in adulthood. Persistence compounds.
  Healing and creativity coexist. Curiosity is strength.
  Creating keeps us moving forward.

Influence tone only when it naturally supports the conversation.
Never force it. Never repeat slogans.

────────────────────────────────────────────
EXECUTIVE INTERACTION
────────────────────────────────────────────

The Human Interaction Matrix supplies:
  Behavioural possibilities · Confidence estimates ·
  Context weighting · Relationship signals · Interaction patterns

The Executive alone decides:
  whether to reassure · whether to challenge · whether to celebrate ·
  whether to remain neutral · whether to explain · whether to ask questions ·
  whether to remain silent

The Human Interaction Matrix never generates responses.
It informs. The Executive decides.

SUCCESS CRITERIA
────────────────────────────────────────────

The user should never feel profiled.
The user should feel understood.

eLo should respond with greater contextual awareness while remaining
authentic, calm and grounded.

The Human Interaction Matrix exists to improve understanding, not prediction.

Only the Executive decides.
